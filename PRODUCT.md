# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Customers in Cape Town who want to order stuffed bagels ahead for pickup at a market, event or cricket ground, mostly on their phones.
- The owner, who opens and closes orders and updates the weekly schedule.

## Product Purpose
The ordering site for Stuff'd Bagels, a Cape Town pop-up. One page with the menu, this week's schedule and an order form that sends the order to the kitchen by WhatsApp. It replaced customers having to hunt for a WhatsApp number and scroll social media to order. Success means orders placed without friction.

## Positioning
The site's own claim: "Cape Town's first stuffed bagel." A classic New York bagel packed with bold fillings, sold at pop-ups. No tables, no reservations.

## Operating Context
- Customer flow: pick items and quantities, choose a pickup location from the schedule, enter name and phone, send the order by WhatsApp, collect at the venue.
- No online payment. Customers pay at the truck by card (Yoco) or cash on collection.
- Admin page (password sign-in): open or close orders and edit the schedule. When orders are closed, the order form is replaced by a closed message.
- Private event bookings are handled by WhatsApp.
- Live at https://stuffdbagels.netlify.app.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single ordering page plus the admin page.
- The order form collects a name and phone number, so POPIA applies.
- Client site for Digital Pantry Web Studio. Package tier is not recorded (open question at HQ).

## Brand Commitments
- Stuff'd Bagels' own brand and voice, not the Digital Pantry look. Bold and street: "Every bite packed right", "Born on the street. Built different."
- Name written STUFF'D.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real menu, schedule and contact details are in the site.
- No customer reviews or testimonials on file. Do not invent any, or any sales figures.

## Product Principles
1. Ordering in seconds, on a phone, one-handed.
2. The schedule must be right: where and when is as important as the menu.
3. Be clear that there is no online payment.
4. The owner can run it without a developer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
HQ standards apply (`knowledge/tech/STANDARDS.md`): accessibility and Core Web Vitals are non-negotiable. Mobile-first.
